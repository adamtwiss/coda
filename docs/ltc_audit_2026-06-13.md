## What the audit KILLED (the load-bearing corrections)

These were live hypotheses going in — including my own framing — and the
deep agents refuted them. Recording so we don't burn fleet on them.

1. **"Novel root-depth-aware pruning-margin damping term."** My earlier
   framing. NO reference engine damps a pruning/reduction MARGIN by global
   root depth (prune-depth + LMR + tree-shape agents independently). It's a
   Coda-unique gamble (`hunt-empirical-not-unique`). The field achieves
   "relax the deep end" through consensus TABLE SHAPE, not a new term. Do
   not build the root-depth term.

2. **"Coda lacks the `ply < 2*rootDepth` extension throttle that all peers
   have" → add it.** WRONG DIRECTION. That throttle makes extensions MORE
   conservative deep; Coda deliberately has none and is thus MORE generous
   deep than all 7 peers — and #221 tested adding the consensus cap →
   **−7.7 Elo H0**. The inverse-lens "extend more in the deep tail" is
   already structurally maxed. Two agents flagged this as the top structural
   gap; the dedicated extensions agent (who instrumented it) demolished it.
   Vindicates waiting for the owning agent before acting.

3. **"Most pruners over-prune at depth."** No — only RFP. Futility and
   SEE-quiet are lmr_depth-scaled (tracks node difficulty, depth-stable),
   LMP/BNFP/razoring are correctly shaped/bounded. Leave them alone.

4. **"Coda may lack a don't-start-a-doomed-iteration predictor."** It has
   one (`2*iter_elapsed` guard), more aggressive than consensus. Not a gap.

5. **Magnitude:** per the #1915 forensics, most of the original ~50-Elo LTC
   gap was TT STARVATION (Hash=64) compensated through SE/LMR micro-knobs;
   the genuine PRUNING-SHAPE residual is only **~10 Elo**. TM is the bigger,
   cleaner lever.

## Search shape — ranked (my lane, ~10 Elo bounded)

### S1. RFP is the lone structural over-pruner at depth — TOP search item
Margin is **linear in RAW depth** (`RFP_MARGIN_NOIMP=43 * depth`), fires to
remaining-depth 16, at only ~38% of the confidence Reckless/PlentyChess
demand deep (430cp vs ~1100cp at remaining-depth 10). "Worst of both
worlds": fires as deep as the quadratic-margin camp but at a linear margin.
Converging evidence:
- prune-depth instrument: RFP cut-rate at fixed remaining-depth GROWS with
  global search depth (rate at rdep≥6 up 1.6-10× STC→LTC); it's the only
  pruner that does (lmr_depth-scaled ones are stable).
- tree-shape #1915 forensics: RFP_MARGIN_NOIMP held/extended high at LTC
  (32→43, healthy hash) — genuine depth effect, not starvation.
- #1874: RFP_DEPTH 18→8 = **+3.5 at LTC** (prior, banked direction).
- **RFP_AUDIT NMP-validation (docs/rfp_nmp_audit_2026-06-10 + Adam
  2026-06-13): ~40% of RFP cutoffs are NOT confirmable by a null-move
  search, rising to ~60% at depth ≥ 8.** This is the strongest single
  signal: deep RFP is cutting nodes a real (null-move) search would NOT cut
  — it's unsound at depth, not merely aggressive. The fix should target
  depth ≥ 8 RFP specifically, and the RFP_AUDIT instrument gives a direct
  success metric: a correct reshape should DROP the unconfirmable-cut rate
  at depth ≥ 8.

Campaign (multi-round, LTC 40+0.4 Hash=256):

### S2. LMR table is the steepest in the field — missing the base-offset DOF
`ln(d)*ln(m)/1.43`, NO base offset. Every peer uses
`base + ln(d)*ln(m)/(larger divisor)` — base lifts shallow, larger divisor
flattens deep. Coda chose gentle-shallow/aggressive-deep. SPSA can't fix it
via the single divisor (trades shallow vs deep); Coda lacks the 2nd DOF.
LMR is the dominant deep-node lever (fires 1700-4600×/Kn at remaining-depth
≥7). Campaign: add LMR_BASE offset + raise LMR_C toward 2.0-3.1, LMR-cluster
retune-on-branch at LTC. Honest caveat: net EFFECTIVE reduction gap is
smaller than the table outlier (runtime adjustments + prior SPSA partly
compensate) → +2-4 Elo or H0. Medium-high direction, modest magnitude.

### S3. Check-conditioned LMR reduction (NOT a check extension)
In-check node fraction rises 7%→12% with remaining depth (instrumented);

LESS in LMR" (`r -= CHECK_LMR_SCALE * gives_check`), bounded — NOT the
full-ply check extension Coda removed for +11.2. Net-new. Modest prior
(QS already resolves most checks via check-gen). Single [0,3] LTC branch,
mandatory adjacent-LMR retune on flat.

## Structural-vs-constant / main-vs-LTC-override (key methodology)
Main stays STC-calibrated; the #1915 LTC values are a deployment override
for codabot. So:
- **STRUCTURAL reshapes** (RFP quadratic margin, LMR base offset) ADD a
  degree of freedom — if STC-neutral and LTC-positive they MERGE TO MAIN and
  improve both regimes. Prioritize these; they fix the over-fit at the root
  rather than forcing a STC↔LTC tradeoff.
- **Constant re-tunes** (FUT_BASE 15→32, ASP_SCORE_DIV) that trade STC for
  LTC belong in the LTC-override deployment set (largely already captured by
  the #1915 mechanism), NOT main.
This distinction decides where each result lands and is why the structural
items outrank the constant ones even at similar raw Elo.
